Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Information collected

We may collect account identifiers, display name, gameplay profile, wallet activity, tournament participation, device presence, and support information.

Payment information

Payments are processed by Razorpay. BattleHub does not store card numbers, UPI PINs, netbanking passwords, or payment credentials. Payment reference records are retained for reconciliation and fraud prevention.

How data is used

Data is used to operate tournaments, maintain balances, prevent fraud, provide support, improve the service, and meet legal obligations.

Data retention and security

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect stored information. Account and payment records should only be retained as necessary for operations, disputes, tax, and regulatory requirements.

User rights

Users may request correction, access, or deletion of applicable personal data through support, subject to legal and transaction-record obligations.
