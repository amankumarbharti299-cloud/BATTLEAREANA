Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Wallet deposits

A successful payment may be automatically credited to the player's wallet. If payment was deducted but wallet credit did not occur, contact support with the payment reference and account ID.

Tournament entry

Entry fees are generally non-refundable once room allocation or match participation has begun. Refunds may be considered for duplicate joins, cancelled tournaments, organizer technical failure, or verified platform errors.

Cancellation by organizer

If BattleHub cancels a tournament before participation begins, the entry fee is credited back to the wallet or refunded to the original payment route as applicable.

Refund timeline

Approved refunds may take 5-10 business days to reflect, depending on the bank or payment provider. Wallet reversals may be processed sooner.

Support

Do not raise an unnecessary duplicate payment dispute while a valid support ticket is active. Include tournament name, account ID, payment ID, order ID, and transaction screenshot (without hiding sensitive card details or PINs).
